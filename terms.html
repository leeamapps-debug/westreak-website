What WeStreak does

WeStreak helps you build habits, on your own or with one other person. You can tick off your habits each day, keep a streak, take breaks, choose a grid colour, and get reminders. When you pair with someone, you share a habit grid and a streak, can send each other one-tap nudges and cheers with fixed wording, and get a weekly recap. There is no free-text chat.

We keep improving WeStreak, so features may change over time. Changes to WeStreak and to these terms, below, explains how.

Pairing with a partner

  1. You can pair with one person at a time by sharing an invite code or link, which lasts 14 days. Only pair with someone who wants to pair with you.
  2. While your pair has Premium, your partner can see your first name, grid colour, the habits you share with them, your ticks and breaks on those habits, your shared streak, milestones and weekly recap, and the nudges and cheers you send. Habits you keep just for yourself stay private. Without Premium, your partner sees only your first name and a preview.
  3. Nudges are limited to three per habit per day, so nobody is overwhelmed.
  4. Either of you can unpair at any time in Settings, with Unpair. When you unpair, the shared streak ends, your shared habits are archived, and you stop seeing each other's information. Each of you carries on with your own habits.
  5. Unpairing also works as a block: your former partner can no longer see your information, and old invites stop working, so they cannot pair with you again unless you invite them.

Free and Premium

Free: track up to three habits on your own. You can pair for free, and while you do, you see the shared side as a preview.

Premium: the full shared grid and streak, streak saves, breaks, nudges and cheers, the weekly recap and milestones, more habits, and six more grid colours. The app shows what each plan includes before you subscribe, and that description forms part of what you are buying.

One plan covers you both

WeStreak Premium is designed for two people. These rules explain how one subscription works for a pair.

  1. Premium belongs to the person who buys it, whom we call the subscriber.
  2. While the subscriber is paired, their partner also gets Premium, at no extra cost. The partner does not pay anything and is not part of the payment agreement.
  3. If the pair ends, for any reason, the subscriber keeps Premium until their subscription ends, and can share it with a new partner. The former partner goes back to the free plan.
  4. If the partner deletes their account, the pair ends and the subscriber keeps Premium.
  5. If the subscriber deletes their account, the pair ends and the partner goes back to the free plan, keeping their own habits. The subscriber's App Store or Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account; the subscriber must cancel it in their store settings, or it will keep renewing.
  6. When Premium ends, because the subscription is cancelled or expires, or a payment fails, there is a 30-day grace period. During it, the pair uses the free plan and the shared streak is frozen and saved. If either of you starts Premium within those 30 days, your streak picks up where it stopped. After 30 days the saved streak ends, and a new streak starts from your next day of Premium.
  7. Only one of you needs to subscribe. If both of you subscribe, each of you is charged separately for your own subscription. If this happens, either of you can cancel in your store settings, and ask Apple or Google for a refund as explained under Your right to cancel and refunds.
  8. Notices about the subscription go to the subscriber, because it is the subscriber's agreement.
