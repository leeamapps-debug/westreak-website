WeStreak support

Get help

Email info@leeamapplications.com and tell us what happened, what phone you use, and which version of WeStreak you have, if you know. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

Cancel your subscription

Deleting the app or your WeStreak account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your store settings, at least 24 hours before it renews or before your free week ends.

On iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then WeStreak, then Cancel Subscription. Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

On Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then WeStreak, then Cancel subscription. Or go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

In WeStreak, the person who pays can also tap Manage subscription in Settings.

To ask for a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play's refund request page.

Restore Premium on a new phone

Sign in with the same account you used before. If Premium does not appear, go to Settings in WeStreak and tap Restore purchases.

Common questions

The invite link did not open the app.

Make sure WeStreak is installed, then open it, tap I have an invite code or link, and type the six-character code or paste the link.

My partner and I use different phones.

WeStreak is launching on iPhone first. Android follows soon after, and partners on iPhone and Android will be able to pair.

Notifications are not arriving.

Check that notifications for WeStreak are switched on in your phone's settings. In WeStreak, a line in Settings tells you if they are off, with a button to open your phone's settings.

How do I unpair?

Go to Settings and tap Unpair. Your shared streak ends, your shared habits are archived, and each of you carries on with your own habits.

Report a problem with your partner

If your partner is using WeStreak to upset, pressure or harass you, unpair straight away in Settings, which stops them seeing your information. Then tell us with Report a problem with your partner in Settings, or by email. We look at every report. If you are in danger, call 999.

Accessibility

WeStreak is built to be used by everyone. One of our founders is blind and uses VoiceOver, and every screen is tested with it. WeStreak works with VoiceOver, follows your phone's text size, and honours Bold Text, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion. Colours are never the only way information is shown. If anything in WeStreak or on this website does not work for you, email info@leeamapplications.com and we will put it right.

Delete your account

See how to delete your account, in the app or without it, at westreakapp.com/delete-account.
