The short version

WeStreak lets two people share habits and a streak. To do that, we store your account, your habits and your daily ticks, and we show some of them to the one partner you choose to pair with. Our database is in Ireland, in the European Union. We do not sell your information, we do not show adverts, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never use your habit titles for analytics. You can switch off anonymous usage statistics in Settings, and you can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

WeStreak is made by Leeam Applications. Leeam Applications is the trading name of Liam O'Rourke and Lee Roberts, in partnership. They are the controller of your personal information, which means they decide how and why it is used and are responsible for looking after it.

What we collect

Information you give us.

  1. Account details. Your email address, and the first name you type during setup. If you sign up with an email and password, we store your password only as a one-way scrambled code, called a hash, never as readable text.
  2. Your habits. The habit titles you type (up to 40 characters), the icons you choose, your reminder times, your daily ticks, any breaks you take, and your chosen grid colour.
  3. Your partner's name. During setup you can type the name of the person you plan to invite, so the app can say "Waiting for" and their name. This is information about someone else, so we keep it only until your pair forms or your invite expires, then delete it.
  4. Messages to us. If you email us or use our forms, we keep what you send so we can reply.

Information created as you use WeStreak.

  1. Pair information. Who you are paired with, when the pair formed, your shared streak, pair days, streak saves, milestones, weekly recaps, and the nudges and cheers you send and receive. Nudges and cheers are fixed, one-tap messages; there is no free-text chat.
  2. Invite codes and links. A six-character code and a link you can share. They expire after 14 days.
  3. Settings. Your time zone, so days and reminders happen at the right time where you are, and your notification choices.
  4. Identifiers. An account number our system creates for you, and a separate random purchase ID that links your subscription to your account without revealing who you are to the app stores.
  5. A push token. A code from your phone that lets us send you notifications.
  6. Security records. Short-lived records that stop people guessing codes or abusing the service, such as one-time sign-in codes and counts of attempts.

Information we get from others.

  1. Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple. Your email address or an Apple Hide My Email relay address, and a unique Apple ID number. We also keep a token from Apple so that, if you delete your account, we can tell Apple to disconnect WeStreak from your Apple ID.
  2. Google, if you use Continue with Google. Your email address and a unique Google ID number. Google may also send your name and profile picture; we do not keep them, because WeStreak asks for your first name during setup.
  3. Apple and Google, about subscriptions. Through our subscription partner Superwall, we learn which plan you are on, whether you are in a free trial, when it ends, when your subscription renews or expires, and which store you used. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  4. Your partner. When your partner invites you, we learn the invite was used and link your accounts.

Information collected automatically.

  1. Anonymous usage statistics. While the Share anonymous usage statistics switch in Settings is on, the app sends a small number of simple events to PostHog, such as "setup finished", "invite sent" and "pair formed". These use a random ID tied to the app on your phone, not your name, email or account, and they never include your habit titles. PostHog is set not to store IP addresses.
  2. Crash and performance reports. If the app crashes or runs slowly, Sentry receives a technical report about what went wrong, such as the device model, operating system version and the part of the app involved. Sentry is set not to collect personal details such as your name, email or IP address, and these reports never include your habit titles.
  3. Subscription screens. Superwall's software in the app receives basic device details, such as the device model, language and app version, so it can show the right subscription screen.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health app data or advertising identifier, and we do not use any tracking for advertising.

What your partner can see

When you pair with someone, they become the only other person who can see your WeStreak information. While your pair has Premium, your partner sees your first name and grid colour; the titles and icons of the habits you share with them; your daily ticks on those habits, your breaks, and your shared streak, pair days, milestones and weekly recap; and the nudges and cheers you send them.

If your pair does not have Premium, your partner sees only your first name and a preview of the shared side, not your habits or ticks. Habits you keep just for yourself are never shown to your partner.

When a pair ends, you stop seeing each other's information. The shared streak ends, your shared habits are archived, and each of you carries on with your own habits. If your partner deletes their account, their name, habits, ticks and messages disappear from your app.

Please only add habit titles you are happy for your partner to read.

A note on habits about your health

You choose what your habits say. Some people track things that relate to their health, such as taking medication or cutting down on alcohol. If you do, that title is stored to run the app and shown to your partner, and we use it for nothing else. We do not read, sort or analyse habit titles, we never send them to our analytics or crash reporting, and we never use them for advertising or marketing. If you would rather keep a health habit private, keep it as a habit just for you, or use a title only you understand.

Why we use your information, and our legal reasons

Data protection law says we need a legal reason, called a lawful basis, for each thing we do with your information.

  1. To create and run your account, sign you in, and send your verification codes by email. Reason: this is necessary to provide WeStreak under our terms with you, which is a contract.
  2. To store your habits and ticks, run streaks, saves, breaks, milestones and weekly recaps, and send the reminders, nudges, cheers and updates you have switched on. Reason: contract.
  3. To pair you with your partner and show them the information described above. Reason: contract. You choose who to pair with, and you can unpair at any time.
  4. To keep the typed partner name until your pair forms. Reason: our legitimate interest in making the invite feel personal, balanced by keeping it only briefly.
  5. To manage your subscription and free week, give Premium to you and your partner, and send service messages about your plan, such as a reminder before your free week ends. Reason: contract.
  6. To keep WeStreak secure, prevent abuse and fraud, and disconnect Sign in with Apple when you delete your account. Reason: our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users.
  7. To find and fix crashes and slow screens. Reason: our legitimate interest in keeping WeStreak working properly.
  8. To understand, in anonymous totals, how WeStreak is used, so we can improve it. Reason: our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can switch this off at any time, as explained under Your right to object.
  9. To keep records we must keep by law, such as tax records, and to answer your requests and complaints. Reason: legal obligation, and our legitimate interest in handling requests properly.

We do not send marketing. If we ever want to, we will ask first. We do not make any decisions about you by purely automatic means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Do you have to give us your information?

To use WeStreak you need an account, which needs an email address, or an Apple or Google sign-in, and a first name. Without them we cannot provide the app. Everything else is up to you: you choose your habits and what they say, whether to pair, and which notifications to allow.

Notifications and emails

Notifications are optional. Your phone asks your permission before WeStreak can send any, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings. Turning them off does not stop you using WeStreak or Premium. Habit reminders are set up on your phone itself. Nudges, cheers, recaps and plan reminders travel through Expo and then Apple's or Google's notification service; they can include your partner's first name, but never your habit titles.

We send emails only for your account and your subscription, such as verification codes, password resets, and important notices about your plan. These are not marketing.

Who helps us run WeStreak

These companies process information for us, only on our instructions, under written agreements that require them to protect it. They are service providers, not companies we sell or share your data with. Each receives only what it needs.

  1. Convex hosts our database and server code in the European Union, on Amazon Web Services in Ireland. Convex, Inc. is based in the United States. It stores everything described in this policy except analytics and crash reports.
  2. Superwall shows our subscription screens and tells us your subscription status. It receives your random purchase ID and your purchase events from Apple or Google. Superwall is based in the United States.
  3. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and iPhone notifications.
  4. Google provides Continue with Google, Google Play payments and Android notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  5. Expo delivers notifications to Apple and Google. It receives your push token and the notification text. Expo is based in the United States.
  6. Resend sends our emails, such as verification codes. It receives your email address and the email's content. Resend is based in the United States.
  7. PostHog provides anonymous usage statistics, hosted in the European Union.
  8. Sentry provides crash and performance reports, hosted in the European Union.
  9. Zoho hosts our email inbox, info@leeamapplications.com, so it holds any email you send us.
  10. GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, hosts westreakapp.com. GitHub may record your IP address in its server logs for security; we do not see or keep those logs.

Apple and Google also act under their own privacy policies when you use their stores, sign-in and accounts.

We would share your information with anyone else only if the law required it, for example a valid court order, to protect someone's safety, or if the business behind WeStreak changed hands or became a company, as explained under If our business changes.

When your information leaves the UK

Our database, usage statistics and crash reports are kept in the European Union, which the UK recognises as giving adequate protection. Some providers are based in the United States and may access or process information there: Convex, Superwall, Expo and Resend. When they do, we rely on these safeguards: for Convex, to be confirmed before WeStreak launches; for Superwall, to be confirmed before WeStreak launches; for Expo, to be confirmed before WeStreak launches; for Resend, to be confirmed before WeStreak launches.

Apple and Google process information worldwide under their own safeguards. Email us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

How long we keep your information

  1. Your account, habits, ticks, pair information and settings: for as long as your account exists.
  2. When you delete your account: we remove it from our live systems straight away. Copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within to be confirmed before WeStreak launches.
  3. Information from a pair that has ended: kept while either of you still has an account, so each of you keeps your own history, and deleted once neither of you does.
  4. The partner name you type during setup: until your pair forms or your invite expires.
  5. Invite codes and links: until they are used, or until they expire after 14 days.
  6. One-time codes and security records: from a few minutes to a few days, then deleted automatically.
  7. Anonymous usage statistics: to be confirmed before WeStreak launches, then deleted.
  8. Crash and performance reports: to be confirmed before WeStreak launches, then deleted.
  9. Subscription information: deleted with your account. Apple and Google keep the records of your payments under their own policies.
  10. Emails and form messages to us, including privacy requests and complaints: up to 2 years after we finish dealing with them, unless we need them longer for a legal claim.

How we protect your information

  1. Encryption in transit. Everything between the app, our servers and our providers travels encrypted.
  2. Encryption at rest. Our database provider encrypts stored data.
  3. Passwords and sign-in. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash. The keys that keep you signed in are held in your phone's secure storage, the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.
  4. Secret keys stay on our servers. The keys that connect WeStreak to its providers are never included in the app.
  5. Partner checks. Every request for a partner's information is checked on our server to confirm you are really paired with them, and that your pair has Premium.
  6. Restricted access. Only Liam O'Rourke and Lee Roberts can access the systems that hold your information, and only when needed to run WeStreak, help you, or fix a problem.
  7. Collecting less. We do not collect what we do not need, and we keep habit titles out of analytics and crash reports.
  8. If something goes wrong. If a security incident affected your personal information, we would tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you; to ask us to correct it if it is wrong; to ask us to delete it, which you can also do yourself; to ask us to limit how we use it; to ask for the information you gave us in a format you can take elsewhere; and to withdraw any consent you have given, at any time.

To use any of these rights, email info@leeamapplications.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address, so that we know it is really you. We will reply within one month, and tell you if we need longer because a request is complicated. There is no charge.

Your right to object. You have the right to object to us using your information where our reason is our legitimate interests. In particular, you can stop anonymous usage statistics at any time by switching off Share anonymous usage statistics in Settings. This is free, takes effect straight away, and does not affect anything else in the app. To object to anything else, email us and we will stop unless we have a compelling reason that the law allows.

Deleting your account. In the app, go to Settings, then Delete account. Deleting your account removes your information as described above and disconnects Sign in with Apple. It does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; please cancel that in your store settings first. If you no longer have the app, see westreakapp.com/delete-account.

Children

WeStreak is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account, and WeStreak is not designed for or directed at children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it. If you think a child is using WeStreak, please tell us at info@leeamapplications.com.

If you are outside the UK

WeStreak is run from the United Kingdom under UK data protection law. Wherever you live, you can use the rights described above by emailing us. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for targeted or cross-context advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We do not respond differently to browser do-not-track or privacy signals, because we do no such tracking.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. Use our privacy complaint form at leeamapplications.com/privacy-complaints, or email info@leeamapplications.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, usually much sooner, look into it, keep you updated, and tell you the outcome in writing.

You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office, at any time, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, by phone on 0303 123 1113, or by post to the Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

Our website

westreakapp.com sets no cookies, has no analytics and no adverts. When you open an invite link, the website hands it to the WeStreak app; the website does not store the invite.

If our business changes

If the business behind WeStreak becomes a limited company owned by its founders, or is sold or merged, your information would pass to the new owner so that WeStreak can carry on. We would tell you before that happened, and update this policy with the new controller's details. The new owner would have to keep using your information in line with this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. This page always shows the current version and its effective date. Earlier versions are available on request.
