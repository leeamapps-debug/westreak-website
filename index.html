Habit tracker for couples and friends: one grid, one shared streak

WeStreak is a habit tracker for two people. You and your partner, or a friend, tick off your habits on one shared grid and keep one streak going together. One plan covers you both.

How WeStreak works

  1. Pick your habits.

    Start with up to three, each with its own icon and a reminder time if you want one.

  2. Invite your person.

    Send them a link, or read out a six-character code. They join from the WeStreak app.

  3. Tick off together.

    Each day you both see your week on one grid. On days you both finish, your shared streak grows.

What you get

  • One shared grid.

    Your last seven days, with both of you on it, each in your own colour.

  • One shared streak.

    It grows on the days you both finish your habits, so you keep each other going.

  • Streak saves.

    Every 14 days in a row together earns a save, up to three. A save covers a day when only one of you managed it.

  • Breaks.

    Going on holiday or feeling unwell? Pause your streak and pick up where you left off.

  • Nudges and cheers.

    One tap sends your partner a friendly nudge or a cheer. Nudges are limited to three per habit a day, so nobody feels hassled. There is no chat to keep up with.

  • Weekly recap.

    A look back at your week together, and milestones to celebrate along the way.

  • Habits just for you.

    Keep some habits private. Your partner only ever sees the ones you share.

Questions

Do we both need to pay?

No. One Premium plan covers you and your partner. Only the person who subscribes is charged.

Can we use WeStreak for free?

Yes. You can track up to three habits on your own and pair with someone for free. Premium opens the shared side.

What does my partner see?

With Premium, your partner sees your first name, the habits you share with them and your ticks on those habits. Habits you keep just for yourself stay private.

Is WeStreak only for couples?

No. It is for any two people: partners, friends, family or gym buddies. You pair with one person at a time.

What if one of us misses a day?

A streak save covers a day when only one of you finished. You earn one every 14 days in a row together, up to three. For a holiday or illness, take a break instead.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your shared streak is frozen and kept for 30 days. Start Premium again within 30 days and it carries on where it stopped.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions. The Manage subscription button in WeStreak's Settings takes you there.

Does WeStreak work on Android?

WeStreak is launching on iPhone first, with Android following soon after.

Does WeStreak work with VoiceOver?

Yes. WeStreak was built and tested with VoiceOver by a founder who uses it every day.
